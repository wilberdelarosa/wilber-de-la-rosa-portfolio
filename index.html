04 / HOW I WORK

no random design.

every step has a job.

I don’t just stitch templates together. We engineer resilient architectures from first principles with autonomous AI departments and verifiable systems.

We start with the offer, map the buying journey, design around the decisions people need to make, then build the d*mn thing properly.

diagnose

Figure out what we’re actually trying to sell.

Offer, audience, traffic, goals, friction, and what’s currently getting in the way.

map

Build the path before building the page.

Pages, sections, messaging hierarchy, CTAs, and how someone moves from interested to ready to buy.

design

Make the strategy impossible to ignore.

Clear hierarchy, strong visuals, intentional UX, and design decisions made around conversion.

build

Turn the pretty file into something that actually works.

Systeme.io, GoHighLevel, or a custom digital build depending on what the project needs.

connect

Make sure nothing falls apart after the click.

Forms, email flows, checkout, CRM, tagging, automation, integrations.

launch

Test it. Break it. Fix it. Then send traffic.

Desktop, mobile, links, forms, automations, and the full customer journey checked before launch.
